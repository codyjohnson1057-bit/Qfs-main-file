THE CORNERSTONE OF THE ALLIANCE IS ANONYMITY

01

OUR COMMITMENT

Privacy is not a feature — it is the foundation. The Robinhood Alliance operates as an anonymous collective. We exist to protect the identity and sovereignty of every participant in the digital sphere.

This Privacy Policy explains what minimal data we handle, how we protect it, and the absolute boundaries of our non-disclosure principles.

02

NON-DISCLOSURE

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties under any circumstances.

Your interaction with our platform is strictly confidential. No external entity receives identifying data from The Alliance.

03

DATA COLLECTION

We collect only the minimal data required to facilitate the protocol:

  • Account details (pseudonymous)
  • Wallet addresses
  • Transaction hashes

This data is pseudonymous by design and cannot be used to identify you in the physical world.

04

NO TRACKING

We do not utilize persistent tracking cookies for behavioral advertising.

We do not build profiles, fingerprint devices, or monitor your activity across the wider web. The Alliance has no interest in surveillance.

05

SECURITY

We employ state-of-the-art encryption and zero-knowledge proofs to ensure that your data remains secure from unauthorized access.

All sensitive operations are designed so that even The Alliance cannot reconstruct your real-world identity from the data we hold.

06

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the privacy practices of those external sites. Access them at your own risk.

07

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. The “Updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest revision.

08

CONTACT

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us exclusively through the secure, anonymous portal on the website.
